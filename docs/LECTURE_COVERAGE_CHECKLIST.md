# Lecture and class material coverage checklist

Use this when finalising the thesis. Tick each box when the listed material appears in your **Word submission** (or equivalent).

## Formatting and topic formation

- [ ] [Formatting guidelines.txt](Lectures%20and%20guides/Formatting%20guidelines.txt): Times New Roman 12, 1.5 spacing, justified body, centred page numbers, APA references, chapter headings centred and bold, table/figure numbering by chapter.
- [ ] [Lec 1 - Topic Formation Lecture Sketch.txt](Lectures%20and%20guides/Lec%201%20-%20Topic%20Formation%20Lecture%20Sketch.txt): Title and abstract align with problem and solution; abstract length and four-part structure (background, problem, solution, method/outcome) addressed in front matter.

## Chapter 1 (see [Lec 2 - Chapter one and Formatting.txt](Lectures%20and%20guides/Lec%202%20-%20Chapter%20one%20and%20Formatting.txt))

- [ ] 1.1 Background with concepts, context, and references (APA in final doc).
- [ ] 1.2 Problem statement (situation, weaknesses of current practice, link to proposed solution).
- [ ] 1.3 General objective (aim).
- [ ] 1.3.2 Specific objectives (SMART-style list).
- [ ] 1.4 Research questions.
- [ ] 1.5 Justification with references.
- [ ] 1.6 Scope and limitations (system limitations, not only personal).
- [ ] Reported speech / third person for the proposed system where the faculty requires it.

## Chapter 2 (see [Lec 3 - Chapter 2.txt](Lectures%20and%20guides/Lec%203%20-%20Chapter%202.txt))

- [ ] 2.1 Introduction mapping sections and literature reviewed.
- [ ] Thematic or objective-linked sections (theory and practice of clinical AI, PHI risk, controls).
- [ ] Related works (minimum comparable systems or product classes; figures or screenshots if required).
- [ ] Gaps in related work leading to your solution.
- [ ] Conceptual framework (diagram in thesis: input, process, output).

## Chapter 3 (see [Lec 4 - Chapter 3.txt](Lectures%20and%20guides/Lec%204%20-%20Chapter%203.txt))

- [ ] 3.1 Introduction naming methodology (OOAD) and chapter map.
- [ ] 3.2 Development approach with **diagram** of phases (Modified Waterfall).
- [ ] 3.3 Justification of methodology choice versus agile-only narratives.
- [ ] 3.4 System analysis: use case and sequence (reference Chapter 4 figures).
- [ ] 3.5 System design preview pointing to Chapter 4 (ERD, schema, class, architecture, mockups if any).
- [ ] 3.6 Deliverables and milestones; Gantt reference to appendix.
